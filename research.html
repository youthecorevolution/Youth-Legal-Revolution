Evidence before conclusions

Legislation & implementation reviews.

This page describes the review method. No case-specific finding is published here yet. A concern is not a finding of illegality or misconduct.

Our review method

  1. Identify the jurisdiction, applicable law, provision and relevant date.
  2. Use primary sources such as legislation, rules, official records and court decisions.
  3. Separate allegations, evidence, legal analysis and verified findings.
  4. Record uncertainty, limitations, reviewer and review date.
  5. Where appropriate, request a response and include it fairly before publication.

Reviews provide public education, not legal advice. Any person affected may contact us to request a correction or submit a response.
