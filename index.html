01 — WHO WE ARE

Justice begins

Youth Legal Revolution is a youth-oriented legal awareness initiative designed to make legal knowledge easier to understand and more accessible to communities.

We encourage constitutional literacy, responsible civic participation, transparency and informed engagement with public institutions.
