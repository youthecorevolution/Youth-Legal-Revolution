# Youth Legal Revolution

Youth Legal Revolution (YLR) is a youth-oriented legal awareness initiative in India. It promotes constitutional literacy, legal education, transparency and responsible civic participation.

## Focus areas
- Legal literacy and public understanding of law
- Constitutional awareness and civic rights
- RTI and transparency
- Public grievance processes and access to justice
- Evidence-based research into laws and public obligations
- Youth participation and community education
