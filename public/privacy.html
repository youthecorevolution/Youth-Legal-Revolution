Privacy information

Last reviewed: 23 November 2026

You can browse public pages without creating an account. The editorial dashboard is restricted to authorized editors and uses a session cookie to keep an authenticated session secure.

Information handled

If you email the initiative, your message and reply address are used to respond. The hosting provider may process standard technical request data as part of operating and protecting the website. Do not send sensitive personal or case information through ordinary email.

Retention and requests

Messages are retained only as needed for the enquiry and record keeping. To ask about a message you sent, write to youthlegalrevolution@gmail.com.

External services

Links to third-party websites are governed by their own privacy practices. This site links to official and social profiles but does not control them.

Review

This privacy page should be reviewed against the actual hosting, analytics, email and retention practices before launch. Contact Youth Legal Revolution with questions.
